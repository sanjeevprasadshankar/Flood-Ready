🧮 Village prioritisation formula

Combined = 0.60 × Risk + 0.40 × Priority
🌊 60% flood risk🤝 40% need & access
  • 👥Population + 2× vulnerable (max 35 pts) — who needs help
  • 🏠Shelter capacity gap (max 25 pts) — can the shelter absorb everyone?
  • 🛣️Road access — cut off = 30, seasonal = 15, all-weather = 0
  • 📵Phone coverage gap (max 10 pts) — how reachable they are

This makes cut-off villages with many vulnerable people rise to the top even when raw flood risk is equal.
